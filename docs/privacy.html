Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers the games and website of Mossgate LLC, doing business as Mossgate Games ("we", "us"), including Gloammere and mossgatellc.com.

The short version

We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Gloammere plays offline and keeps your save files on your own device.

Information our games collect

Gloammere does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not include advertising or analytics tools. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, those saves are deleted.

Google Play

When you download or buy a game through Google Play, Google processes that transaction under Google's privacy policy. We receive only the reports Google provides to developers, such as sales totals and anonymous crash and performance statistics. These do not identify you to us.

This website

mossgatellc.com is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep the service secure; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to provide support. We do not add you to mailing lists or share your email.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If a future version of a game adds features that handle personal data, such as cloud saves or online play, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
