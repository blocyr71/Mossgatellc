Gloammere

The valley has gone quiet. Towns have shuttered their windows, roads have grown over, and something walks the fog after dark. You come home with a lantern, a hound, and a handful of strange creatures willing to follow you. What you build from here is up to you.

Gloammere is a dark, storybook monster-taming RPG. Tend a home, raise a crew of loyal creatures, and explore a gothic valley where every lamp you relight pushes the dark back a little further.

Befriend, don't just catch

Dozens of creatures roam the valley, from bone hounds to moss-backed tortoises. Win them over, grow their bond, and they'll fight beside you, carry you across rivers, and open paths you could never reach alone.

Put your souls to work

Your creatures aren't just for battle. Staff the Works at your farm: forges, kilns, looms and mills, linked by haul lines your creatures run themselves. Each soul has traits, earns ranks and gets better at the job it loves.

Rebuild a broken valley

Every town has projects waiting: a wharf on stilts, a granary roof, lamps along the mill road. Supply them and watch the town change, from scaffolding to lit windows and people back on the streets.

Explore what lies beneath

Crypts with coffins that shouldn't be opened. Mines that go deeper every floor. Smugglers' caves, ice caverns, catacombs, flooded tunnels. Hundreds of hand-built places, each one lit by your lantern alone.

A world that turns

Day fades to dusk and lamps flicker on. Rain sweeps the harbour, fog rolls through the graves, snow settles on the rooftops. Seasons change the land, and the townsfolk keep their own routines.

A story with teeth

Old bargains, nobles who keep their word to the letter, a village that has forgotten who its festival was for. A hand-written tale told in four acts, with choices that change who stands with you at the end.
